RH Color Field

Every integer is an emitter blending its color wave into one field. Where the blend cancels perfectly — that's a zeta zero. Watch the zeros form as you add emitters, one by one.

Ford Circles

Every fraction gets a circle. Circles kiss their Farey neighbors and never cross — and how evenly they spread is, by the Franel–Landau theorem, exactly as strong a question as RH itself.

What these are, honestly: visualizations and pedagogy. The classical theorems they illustrate (Ford's tangency theorem, the Franel–Landau equivalence, series cancellation) are proved mathematics; the IMM framework itself is a research program whose claims carry explicit, graded status labels. Nothing on these pages is evidence for or against the Riemann Hypothesis, and none of it claims to be.
